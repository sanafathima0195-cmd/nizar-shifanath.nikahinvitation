# 💍  Wedding Invitation Website

A luxury digital wedding invitation webpage inspired by the **Royal Majesty** design, tailored in **Deep Royal Navy & Champagne Gold** with personalized couple photos and custom venue details.

---

## 📍 Wedding Details Configured
- **Venue:** Shadi Mahal at Painery Auditorium, Kunjila (Kodagu / Coorg, Karnataka)
- **Palette:** Deep Royal Navy (`#040813`) & Champagne Gold (`#d4af37`)
- **Photos:** Custom portraits of the Groom & Bride in ornate royal frames

---

## ✨ Features Included

1. **Royal 3D Gate Reveal Overlay:**
   - 3D door opening animation upon clicking "Open Invitation"
   - Monogram royal wax seal crest
2. **Harmonious Ambient Music (Web Audio API):**
   - Soothing, royal instrumental melodic progression that begins smoothly when the invitation is opened
   - Floating audio toggle button on top right to play/pause anytime
3. **Couple Showcase:**
   - Groom card with photograph, parents' blessings, and personalized quote
   - Bride card with photograph, parents' blessings, and personalized quote
   - Interlocking golden wedding rings animation
4. **Interactive "Save The Date" Scratch Card:**
   - Scratch-to-reveal golden foil canvas with mouse or touch
   - Automatically reveals date & time when scratched past 40% (or via one-click reveal button)
5. **Live Countdown Timer:**
   - Accurate real-time Days, Hours, Minutes, and Seconds countdown
6. **Wedding Events & Itinerary:**
   - Sacred Nikah ceremony schedule
   - Walima & Grand Feast details
7. **Venue & Navigation:**
   - Embedded interactive Google Map for **Painery Auditorium, Kunjila**
   - "Get Directions on Google Maps" button
   - "Add to Calendar" (Google Calendar / iCal) link
8. **Photo Gallery & Memories:**
   - High-resolution showcase of the couple with smooth hover zoom effects
9. **Interactive RSVP & Guestbook Wall:**
   - Direct RSVP sending via WhatsApp to the couple/hosts
   - Live on-page guest wishes feed
10. **In-Browser Quick Customizer Modal:**
    - Click the **"Edit Details"** badge at the top right to instantly change Groom/Bride names, Wedding Date, Time, Venue, and WhatsApp number right in your browser!
